💊 Weight Loss Medication Check
Find out if medications like Ozempic, Wegovy, or Mounjaro may be right for you — and whether your insurance will cover it.
How do these medications work?
GLP-1 medications (like Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Zepbound) help reduce appetite, stabilize blood sugar, and support significant weight loss. Many insurance plans cover them — but eligibility depends on your health profile.

Pulmonology · Sleep Medicine
😴 Sleep Apnea Screening
Sleep apnea affects 1 in 4 adults and most don't know they have it. This quick check uses a clinically validated screening tool.
Signs you might have sleep apnea
Loud snoring, gasping for air, waking up exhausted, or being told you stop breathing during sleep are all warning signs. Untreated sleep apnea raises your risk for heart disease, diabetes, and stroke.

Endocrinology · Bone Health
🦴 Bone Health & Osteoporosis Check
Osteoporosis causes bones to become weak and break easily — often with no warning signs until a fracture happens.
Did you know?
50% of women and 25% of men over age 50 will break a bone due to osteoporosis. Most people don't know their bones are weak until it's too late. This check takes 1 minute.

Palliative & Supportive Care
🕊️ Is Palliative Care Right for You?
Palliative care is not just for end of life. It's about living as fully as possible — at any stage of a serious illness.
Common misconception: Palliative care ≠ Hospice
Palliative care can be received alongside any treatment — surgery, chemotherapy, dialysis, or other therapies. It focuses on comfort, quality of life, and support for you and your family.

Infectious Disease · HIV Prevention
🛡️ HIV Prevention (PrEP) Check
PrEP is a highly effective medication that prevents HIV. Find out if the daily pill or a bi-monthly injection may be right for you.
What is PrEP?
PrEP (Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis) is a safe, FDA-approved medication for people who don't have HIV but may be at risk. When taken as directed, PrEP reduces the risk of getting HIV from sex by about 99%. There are now two options: a daily pill or an injection every 2 months.
💊
Daily Pill
Truvada · Descovy
💉
Bi-Monthly Injection
Apretude · every 2 months
🔒 This screening is completely private and non-judgmental. PrEP is for anyone who may be at risk — regardless of gender, orientation, or lifestyle.
